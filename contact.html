FaQ

Frequently asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend booking online to guarantee your spot during busy periods like weekends and school holidays.

What ages is Kids Play Zone suitable for?

Our play zones are designed for children aged 1-12. We have a dedicated toddler soft play area for ages 1-4 and more challenging zones for older kids.

Can I bring my own food and cake?

Yes! For general play sessions, you're welcome to bring your own snacks. For birthday parties, you can bring a cake, or we can arrange catering for you.

Is there a dress code for play?

We recommend comfortable clothing and non-slip socks. Socks are required in all play areas for hygiene and safety. We sell them at reception if you forget.

Do you offer refunds for party bookings?

Yes, we offer a full refund for cancellations made at least 14 days before your party date. For cancellations within 14 days, we can reschedule your party.
