Frequently asked Questions

What should my child wear to the play zone?

Comfortable clothes and non-slip socks are required. We sell socks at the front desk if you forget. Long hair should be tied back for safety.

Can I reschedule or cancel a booking?

Yes, free rescheduling and cancellation up to 12 hours before your session from the "My Bookings" tab. Late cancellations consume 1 visit from your pass.

Is there a café at the play zone?

Yes! Our parent café serves coffee, snacks, and healthy options. Free WiFi is available for parents while children play.

How do birthday party bookings work?

Choose a package, pick a date, and our party coordinator will contact you within 24 hours to plan all the details including decorations, food, and activities.
